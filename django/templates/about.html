About the Attendance System

About the Attendance System This attendance system, developed by graduates of the University of Imam Al-Sadiq , utilizes fingerprint technology to provide a secure and efficient method for tracking student attendance. It aims to streamline the attendance process for both students and faculty, ensuring accurate data and reducing administrative burdens.
